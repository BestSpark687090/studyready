Unit conversion basics

Always track units through a calculation
Write units next to every number and cancel them like fractions - if the units left over don't match what the question asks for, a step is wrong.
SI base units to know
Meter (length), kilogram (mass), second (time), ampere (current), kelvin (temperature). Most other units are combinations of these.
Common prefixes
kilo- (×1000), centi- (×0.01), milli- (×0.001) - memorize these and you can convert almost any metric unit.
