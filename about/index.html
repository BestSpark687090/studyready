About

Why StudyReady exists

A free resource built for students who want to study more effectively without paying for it.

StudyReady started from a simple observation: most students study hard but not efficiently. They re-read textbooks, highlight everything, and cram the night before - habits that feel productive but don't actually work that well. Meanwhile, the research on what does work is sitting in academic papers most students will never read.

This site is an attempt to bridge that gap. Every tool and tip here is grounded in what learning science actually says about memory, retention, and focus - translated into something practical you can use today.

There's no subscription, no account, and no paywall. Students shouldn't have to pay to learn how to learn.

What we believe

Free, always

Every tool and resource on this site is free to use. No premium tier, no trial period, no credit card.

No account required

You shouldn't have to hand over an email address to use a study tool. Nothing here requires sign-up.

Evidence-based

The strategies we recommend come from cognitive science research, not productivity influencers.

Works for everyone

Whether you're in middle school, high school, or college, the fundamentals of learning are the same.

StudyReady is an independent project. If you find it useful, share it with a classmate.
