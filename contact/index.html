Get in touch

Bug reports, corrections, and requests all go to the same inbox.

StudyReady is an independent project maintained by one person, so you will get a reply from a human, not a ticketing system. The best way to reach us is email:

hello@interactiveextendedlearning.org

Replies usually take a few days. Please include the page URL if you are reporting a problem.

What we are happy to hear about

  • Something broken. A tool that does not save, a formula that is wrong, a link that goes nowhere. Tell us the page and, if it is a tool, which browser you are using.
  • Corrections. The subject reference pages are checked, but mistakes slip through. If you are a teacher or a student who spotted an error, we want to know.
  • Requests. A subject you wish we covered, a tool you wish existed, or a guide you would find useful. We prioritize based on what people actually ask for.
  • Teachers and schools. If you want to use StudyReady tools in a classroom, you do not need permission, but we would love to hear how it goes and what would make it work better for you.

What we cannot help with

  • We do not do homework, write essays, or answer assignment questions. The tools and guides are meant to help you do that work yourself.
  • We cannot recover data from a tool if you cleared your browser storage. Everything is saved locally on your device (see the privacy policy), so there is no copy on our end to restore.

Advertising and partnerships

Ads on the site are served through Google AdSense. We do not sell ad placements directly, publish sponsored posts, or accept payment for links, so please do not email about those.
