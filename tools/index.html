Study Tools

Tools for every part of studying

From memorization to time management, these free tools cover the full study workflow - no account needed.

Memorization

Retain what you learn

Flashcards

Flashcard Builder

Create and review digital flashcards using spaced repetition - the most effective method for long-term memorization.

 Self-Testing

Practice Quiz Generator

Enter your notes as question-and-answer pairs and generate a multiple choice quiz instantly. Great for exam prep.

 Vocabulary

Word List Driller

Build vocabulary lists for any subject - foreign languages, science terms, history names - and drill them on repeat.

Organization

Keep your notes and time in order

Note-Taking

Cornell Notes Template

The Cornell method splits notes into cues, content, and summary - proven to improve retention and review speed.

 Planning

Study Planner

Break assignments into daily sessions. Set deadlines, mark priorities, and track what's done.

 Mind Mapping

Concept Map Builder

Visualize how ideas connect. Useful for understanding complex topics in science, history, or literature.

Focus

Stay on task

Timer

Pomodoro Timer

25 minutes of focused work followed by a 5-minute break. Repeat. One of the most popular productivity techniques worldwide.

 Writing

Essay Outline Tool

Structure your argument before you start writing. Build a thesis, supporting paragraphs, and a conclusion that holds together.

 Distraction

Focus Checklist

A simple pre-study checklist: phone away, water nearby, notifications off, goal written down. Takes 60 seconds.
